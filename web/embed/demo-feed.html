How to put one in a post 
<div class="enjin-embed"
     data-game="/demoroom/playground/"
     data-title="Playground"
     data-poster="/posters/playground.jpg"></div>
<script src="/embed/enjin-embed.js" defer></script>
data-poster is optional; without it the card draws a gradient. data-hint sets the caption, data-aspect the shape (default 16/9). Press Esc to close a running game.
